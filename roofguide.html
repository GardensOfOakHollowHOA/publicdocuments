Approved Roof Guide

The following roofing options are provided as reference for homeowners planning roof replacement or repair. Please review the product information and submit your request through the Association’s design review process before beginning work with a roof replacement.

The design guidelines allow for roof replacement using concrete coated metal tiles from Decra or Tilcor. The specific approved tiles and colors are shown below. The HOA has physical samples of the approved materials you can view yourself. Just reach out to the Board.

How to Begin

A new roof is a major investment in your home. To begin you will need to select a roofing contractor and then submit a Design Review form to the Design Review Committee to verify you are installing an approved roof. We want to take the stress out of roof replacement approval so you can begin enjoying the benefits of a new roof.
